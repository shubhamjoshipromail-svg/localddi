Not for clinical use.

localddi is NOT a medical device and is NOT clinically validated. Results come from third-party research data that may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. The absence of a listed interaction does not mean a combination is safe. Do not use this output to make treatment decisions; consult a qualified clinician and an approved drug reference.
